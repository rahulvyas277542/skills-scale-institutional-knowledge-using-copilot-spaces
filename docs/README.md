# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains the shared project management guidance for OctoAcme. It is designed to help teams move from idea to execution in a repeatable, transparent way while keeping customer value, quality, and risk management at the center of delivery.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project management approach with clear ownership, measurable outcomes, and data-informed decisions. Work moves through a consistent lifecycle:

1. Initiation — validate the business problem, align stakeholders, define success metrics, and decide whether the work should proceed.
2. Planning — translate the initiative into a backlog, milestones, owner assignments, dependencies, and an agreed Definition of Done.
3. Execution and tracking — manage day-to-day delivery through project boards, standups, pull requests, risk monitoring, quality checks, and progress reporting.
4. Release and deployment — confirm readiness, deploy safely, verify outcomes, and communicate releases to stakeholders.
5. Retrospective and continuous improvement — capture learnings after sprints, milestones, or incidents and turn them into actionable improvements.

Project Managers coordinate schedules, dependencies, communications, and risk management. Product Managers define outcomes and priorities. Developers and QA build and validate increments. Stakeholders provide input, approvals, and business context. Together, these roles use backlogs, acceptance criteria, dashboards, demos, checklists, risk registers, and retrospectives to keep work aligned and high quality.
